## Explanation

The initial <py>s</py> sounds like it does in English.  The initial
<py>sh</py> sounds like it does in Engish, with the tongue curved up a
little more.  The initial <py>r</py> sounds like it does in English,
except maybe a little more forcefully, and sometimes with a little bit
of a buzz.

Click on the buttons to hear these three initials followed by the
final <egpy>eng</egpy>.

<listen syl="sēng shēng rēng"></listen>

**Note:** The initials <py>s sh r</py> never combine with finals
beginning with <py>y</py>.
