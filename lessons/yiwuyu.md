## Explanation

We start with the syllables that have no initial and just one vowel sound.  How they are spelled as may seem 
confusing at first, but we will give you some hints on how to remember them.

The first syllable is <py>yi</py>.  It sounds like the _i_ in the English word <eg src="machine">machine</eg>.  In 
_pinyin_, <py>y</py> _before_ <py>i</py> _is always silent_.  Also, <py>i</py> _never begins a 
syllable_; if you have the urge to begin a syllable with <py>i</py>, you'll need a <py>y</py> to help you out.
