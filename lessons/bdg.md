## Explanation (1)

_Pinyin_ has the initials <py>b</py> <py>d</py> <py>g</py>, but
they don't make the same sounds as the English letters _b_
_d_ _g_.

Pronounce the following pairs of English words:

&nbsp;<eg src="pill">pill</eg>&nbsp;<eg src="spill">spill</eg>

&nbsp;<eg src="till">till</eg>&nbsp;<eg src="still">still</eg>

&nbsp;<eg src="kill">kill</eg>&nbsp;<eg src="skill">skill</eg>

You might not have noticed it before, but the _p t k_ in the
words in the first column are _not_ pronounced like the _p t
k_ in the words in the second column.  The _p t k_ in the
words in the first column are _aspirated_; there's a little puff
of air or an _h_ sound following them.

You can prove this to yourself in a couple of ways.  If you hold a
piece of paper close to your lips, it will move when you say the words
in the first column, but not when you say the words in the second
column.  (The paper moves the most for <eg src="pill">pill</eg>.)  Or you can
hold a mirror in front of your mouth; it will fog up for the words in
the first column, but not for the words in the second column.

## Explanation (2)

The initials <py>b d g</py> are the unaspirated companions to the
initials <py>p t k</py>.  <py>b</py> sounds like the _p_ in
<eg src="spill">spill</eg>, <py>d</py> sounds like the _t_ in <eg src="still">still</eg>,
and <py>g</py> sounds like the _k_ in <eg src="skill">skill</eg>.

Click on the following buttons to hear these three initials followed
by <egpy>a</egpy> in the first tone.

<listen syl="bā dā gā"></listen>

At first it might seem that <py>b d g</py> sound like English _b d
g_.  There is a difference: the English letters _b d g_ are
_voiced_; the _pinyin_ letters <py>b d g</py> are
_unvoiced_.

To see the difference, put your finger on your Adam's apple and say
the following pairs of words:

&nbsp;<eg src="bill">bill</eg>&nbsp;<eg src="spill">spill</eg>

&nbsp;<eg src="dill">dill</eg>&nbsp;<eg src="still">still</eg>

&nbsp;<eg src="gill">gill</eg>&nbsp;<eg src="skill">skill</eg>

Your Adam's apple starts to vibrate on the consonants _b d g_ in
the words in the first column, but it doesn't vibrate until you get to
the _i_ in the words in the second column.

**The majority of initials come in aspirated-unaspirated pairs.**
