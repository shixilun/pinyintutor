## Explanation

These two syllables are exceptions to how the letter <py>a</py>
sounds.

The syllable <py>yan</py> sounds like the English word <eg src="yen">yen</eg>.
It begins with the <egpy>yi</egpy> sound, but it does _not_ end
with the <egpy>an</egpy> sound.

Click on the buttons to hear <py>yan</py> in the four tones.

Remember to write <py>yan</py>, not _yen_.

The syllable <py>yuan</py> rhymes with the English word <eg src="can">can</eg>.
It begins with the <egpy>yu</egpy> sound, but it does _not_ end
with the <egpy>an</egpy> sound.
