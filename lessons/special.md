## er

The letter combination <py>er</py> is special in _pinyin_.  It
doesn't combine with any initials.  It sounds like the English word
<eg src="are">are</eg>.

Click on the buttons to hear the syllable <py>er</py> in the four
tones.

## zhi chi shi ri zi ci si

The initials <py>zh ch sh r</py> can be followed by an _r_ sound.
The initials <py>z c s</py> can be followed by a buzzing sound.  Both
of these sounds are represented by the letter <py>i</py>.  **This use
of the letter** <py>i</py> **does not come from the final**
<egpy>yi</egpy>**.**

Originally the committee was going to use the letter &#x026A;,
which is not found in English but used in other 
languages, to represent both of these sounds, so that
<py>i</py> would represent only the <egpy>yi</egpy> sound.  However, it
was later decided that _pinyin_ should not add any extra letters
to the standard twenty-six letters of the Roman alphabet, 
so <py>i</py> is used for all three sounds, and
you have to learn another exception to the rules.

Click on the buttons to hear these seven syllables in the four tones.

<tonerows rawsyl="zhi chi shi ri"></tonerows>

<tonerows rawsyl="zi ci si"></tonerows>
