## Explanation

The vowels <py>a</py> <py>e</py> <py>o</py> form the only syllables
with only one letter.

The syllable <py>a</py> --- that means an <py>a</py> _all by
itself_ --- is pronounced like the _a_ in the English word
<eg src="father">father</eg>.

Click on the buttons below to hear the syllable <py>a</py> in its
four tones.

<tonerows rawsyl="a"></tonerows>

The syllable <py>e</py> --- that means an <py>e</py> _all by
itself_ --- sounds a little like the English word <eg src="uh">uh</eg>.  You
may be tempted to write it as _uh_, but don't do that.

The syllable <py>o</py> --- that means an <py>o</py> _all by
itself_ --- sounds like the letter _o_ in the English word
<eg src="hole">hole</eg>.  It does not sound like the English word <eg src="oh">oh</eg>;
we will find out later why.

Click on the buttons below to hear the syllables <py>e</py> and
<py>o</py> in the four tones.
