## Explanation

Listen to the following two sentences.

<egsentence src="itsal">It's Al.</egsentence>

<egsentence src="itshal">It's Hal.</egsentence>

The only difference between these two sentences is the _h_ sound
in the second.

The letter <py>z</py> is pronounced like the _ts_ sound in "It's
Al."  You can also think of it as a _pinyin_ <py>d</py> followed
by a _pinyin_ <py>s</py>.

The letter <py>c</py> is pronounced like the _ts_ + _h_
sound in "It's Hal."  You can also think of it as a _pinyin_
<py>t</py> followed by a _pinyin_ <py>s</py>.

This is one of the most subtle differences you'll have to learn to
distinguish.

Below are some pairs you can listen to.  See if you can hear the
difference.  You might want to listen to the two English sentences
above for comparison.

<listen syl="zā cā"></listen>
<listen syl="zè cè"></listen>
<listen syl="zǎi cǎi"></listen>

**Note:** The initials <py>z</py> and <py>c</py> never combine with
finals beginning with <py>y</py>.
