## Explanation (1)

The letters <py>m f n l</py> sound the same in English and Chinese.

The letter <py>h</py> sounds as it does in English, though maybe a
little rougher.

Click on the following buttons to hear these three initials followed
by <py>a</py> in the first tone.

<listen syl="mā fā nā lā"></listen>
<listen syl="hā"></listen>

## Explanation (2)

The initials <py>n</py> and <py>l</py> can be followed by <py>yu</py>
and <py>yue</py>.  When this happens, the <py>yu</py> is changed to
<py>ü</py>.  **This is the only time you will ever see** <py>ü</py>
**in** _pinyin_.

Click on the buttons below to hear these four syllables.

<tonerows rawsyl="nü lü nüe lüe"></tonerows>

To type in <py>ü</py>, first type <py>u</py> and then type <py>:</py>
(a colon).  You add the tones the same way as before.  Try typing
<py>nü lü nüe lüe</py> in the four tones in the box below.

**NEED BOX HERE**
